Privacy Policy for Sponskip

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Core Privacy Commitment

Sponskip is built on a single uncompromising principle: your browsing data belongs strictly to you. Sponskip does not collect, log, track, share, or sell any personal data, viewing history, account credentials, or telemetry of any kind.

2. What Data We Process

  • YouTube Video Captions: When you open a YouTube video, Sponskip reads the video's public caption track in your browser. This analysis runs 100% locally on your machine using an on-device linguistic scanner. The video text is never transmitted to any third-party server.
  • Public Crowdsourced Timestamps: To provide instant skips for previously analyzed videos, Sponskip may query the public SponsorBlock API using the public YouTube Video ID. No personal identifier, IP log, or cookies are shared.
  • Local Usage Statistics: Counters such as total ads skipped and estimated time saved are stored exclusively on your device inside Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never synced, transmitted, or accessible to anyone else.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

Sponskip does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or remote telemetry tools. It has zero external AI API dependencies and functions entirely client-side.

4. Permissions Required

  • storage: Used solely to remember your preferred settings (auto-skip toggle, audio chime) and keep your local skipped stats counter on your device.
  • Host Permissions (*://*.youtube.com/*): Necessary to detect the YouTube player element, read closed caption tracks for sponsor phrases, and seek forward when an ad plays.

5. Contact

For questions or support regarding this policy, reach out at support@sponskip.com or visit our GitHub organization at github.com/sponskip.
