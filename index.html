Why ordinary ad blockers fail

YouTubers and podcasters know ad blockers exist, so they weave paid sponsorships directly into the interview conversation. Here's how Sponskip beats them:

↩️

Backward Trace Detection

Sneaky hosts start with an organic story before revealing a promo code. Sponskip detects the pitch cluster and traces backwards up to 45 seconds to catch the disguised setup.

⚡

Zero Audio Transcription Lag

No heavy Whisper models draining your Mac battery. Sponskip reads YouTube's signed caption stream in under 200ms the millisecond the tab opens.

🔒

100% Private & Client-Side

Zero browsing history leaves your machine. Pattern matching runs entirely in local Chrome memory with zero analytics and zero tracking.

Install in 30 Seconds

  1. 1 Download or clone the repo from github.com/sponskip/sponskip
  2. 2 Navigate to chrome://extensions in Chrome and toggle on Developer mode.
  3. 3 Click Load unpacked, select the sponskip folder, and you're done!
